## UI

Vide from `@rbxts/vide`. Give each component worth looking at a story beside it, `<component>.story.tsx`, and look at its picture: `mise run ui` renders every story offline. Before you build, restyle or check UI, read `.agents/skills/build-ui/SKILL.md`: mounting the UI root, writing stories, looking at their pictures on any screen size, clicking into them, and before and after.
